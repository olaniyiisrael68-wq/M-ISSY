About M-ISSY

Your No.1 Tech Store — Quality Guaranteed.

Our Story

M-ISSY started with a simple goal: to provide Nigerians with high-quality, authentic technology products without the hassle of traditional retail.

We noticed that many customers struggle with getting genuine gadgets at fair prices, combined with reliable after-sales support. That's why we adopted a direct-to-customer approach using WhatsApp—making the buying process as easy as sending a message to a friend.

Today, M-ISSY is a trusted name for everything from the latest smartphones and premium laptops to home appliances like TVs and fridges.

Our Mission

To empower our customers by providing seamless access to the world's leading technology brands, backed by transparent pricing, unmatched convenience, and highly responsive customer service.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted and customer-centric electronics retailer in Nigeria, setting the standard for quality assurance and digital shopping convenience.
