Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to M-ISSY Tech Store. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This privacy policy will inform you as to how we look after your personal data when you visit our website and tell you about your privacy rights and how the law protects you.

2. The Data We Collect

Because we process orders directly through WhatsApp, we do not require you to create an account or provide sensitive payment information on our website. However, we may collect, use, store, and transfer different kinds of data, including:

  • Identity Data: includes your name and WhatsApp username when you contact us.
  • Contact Data: includes your WhatsApp phone number and delivery address provided during the ordering process on WhatsApp.
  • Technical Data: includes internet protocol (IP) address, browser type and version, time zone setting, and operating system when you browse our site.

3. Google AdSense and Cookies

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements on our website. Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies to serve ads on our site.

Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Alternatively, you can opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting www.aboutads.info.

4. How We Use Your Data

We will only use your personal data when the law allows us to. Most commonly, we will use your personal data in the following circumstances:

  • To process and deliver your order via WhatsApp.
  • To manage our relationship with you, including asking you to leave a review.
  • To use data analytics to improve our website, products/services, marketing, customer relationships, and experiences.

5. Third-Party Links

This website may include links to third-party websites, plug-ins, and applications (such as WhatsApp). Clicking on those links or enabling those connections may allow third parties to collect or share data about you. We do not control these third-party websites and are not responsible for their privacy statements.

6. Your Legal Rights

Under certain circumstances, you have rights under data protection laws in relation to your personal data, including the right to request access, correction, erasure (right to be forgotten), and restriction of processing.
