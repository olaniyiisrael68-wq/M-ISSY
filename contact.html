Frequently Asked Questions

How do I place an order?

Ordering is simple! Browse our shop, add items to your cart, and click "Checkout via WhatsApp". Alternatively, you can click "Order on WhatsApp" directly from any product page. This will open a chat with us where we finalize your payment and delivery details.

What payment methods do you accept?

We process all payments via direct bank transfer or authorized payment links sent to you securely via our official WhatsApp lines.

How long does delivery take?

Deliveries within Lagos typically take 24-48 hours. Outside Lagos, delivery takes 3-5 business days depending on your location. We provide tracking information via WhatsApp once dispatched.

Are your products authentic?

Absolutely. We guarantee that all our products are 100% authentic, brand-new, and sourced directly from verified manufacturers or their authorized distributors. They come with standard manufacturer warranties.

What is your return policy?

We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for factory defective items. The item must be in its original packaging with all accessories. Please contact our WhatsApp support to initiate a return.
